# Atharva's Little Delivery Adventure

## Platform
web

## Stack
User specified Vite, TypeScript, Three.js, and GSAP; procedural assets, no external editor or backend.

## Purpose and users
A family records a polished newborn name reveal for television. The deliverable is a locally runnable cinematic toy-world experience celebrating ATHARVA.

## Confirmed constraints
The latest update requests an infinite loop and permits a longer duration, superseding the earlier non-looping 60-second endpoint. Each cycle is 72 seconds: retain the reveal and full-name hold at 45–60 seconds, dissolve the actors and balloons at 60–65, reset invisibly, then repeat. Landscape 16:9, 1920 × 1080 export, stationary orthographic camera, 5% safe margins. Start paused; deterministic seeking and restart; independent ambient motion. Provide fullscreen and one-cycle browser WebM export plus MP4 conversion instructions. Audio is optional.

## Visual commitments
Soft, detailed, pastel low-poly miniature island, with stronger environmental and title contrast. Charming cottage, stone delivery path, procedural courier riding a bicycle, seven letter balloons forming a staggered upward arc. Shared directional wind, scrolling recycled clouds, visible ivory/gray birds, varied flowers, and small purple-to-magenta butterflies. The courier lifts the parcel lid before a golden burst. UI stays outside the video.

## Open decisions
No audio source was supplied. The implementation is silent. No hosting or publication was requested.

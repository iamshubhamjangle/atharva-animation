# Atharva's Little Delivery Adventure

## Platform
web

## Stack
User specified Vite, TypeScript, Three.js, and GSAP; procedural assets, no external editor or backend.

## Purpose and users
A family records a polished newborn name reveal for television. The deliverable is a locally runnable cinematic toy-world experience celebrating ATHARVA.

## Confirmed constraints
The final production brief supersedes the earlier portrait and 25-second instructions. Exactly 60 seconds, landscape 16:9, 1920 × 1080 export, stationary orthographic camera, 5% safe margins. Start paused; deterministic seeking and restart; independent ambient motion. Keep the final name visible for 45–60 seconds and hold the final frame. Provide fullscreen and browser WebM export plus MP4 conversion instructions. Audio is optional.

## Visual commitments
Soft, detailed, pastel low-poly miniature island. Charming cottage, stone delivery path, procedural courier and cart, seven letter balloons. UI stays outside the video.

## Open decisions
No audio source was supplied. The implementation is silent. No hosting or publication was requested.
